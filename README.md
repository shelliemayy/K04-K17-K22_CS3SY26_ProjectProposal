Project Description:

Our project is an RPG Dating Simulator designed to provide users with a relaxing and entertaining outlet through interactive storytelling. Players control "Y/N," an alpha female in her mid-20s who explores her relationship options through five partners, each with distinct personalities and responses.

The system consists of a parent class, Partner, which contains shared attributes such as the partner's name and love meter, and methods such as interact(), update_love_meter(), and check_relationship_level() for managing interactions and relationship progression. Five subclasses inherit from this parent class, each overriding interact() to implement unique responses based on their respective personalities. The player makes choices that influence their partner's love meter, which consists of four levels (0–3). Each level unlocks new interactions between the characters.

The expected outputs include character responses, love meter updates, and different story endings. Reaching Level 3 unlocks a proposal option, resulting in a good ending, while reaching Level 0 results in a breakup and a bad ending.
The application targets teenagers and young adults who enjoy role-playing games, dating simulators, and interactive storytelling, particularly casual players seeking an accessible form of entertainment.

Goals:

In the long term, the project aims to provide users with an accessible and engaging form of entertainment through interactive storytelling and personalized gameplay. It also seeks to encourage creativity and exploration of different fictional personalities and relationship dynamics while demonstrating the practical application of object-oriented programming concepts.

Objective 1: Demonstrate object-oriented programming principles, particularly inheritance and polymorphism, through the parent and partner subclasses. 

Objective 2: Develop five distinct partner subclasses with unique personalities and dialogue to provide varied gameplay experiences. 

Objective 3: Implement a functional love meter system that responds to player choices and unlocks interactions and story endings based on relationship progression.
